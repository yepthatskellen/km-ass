Philosophy

Prolonged contact
changes everything.

The most powerful brand relationships are built the same way the most powerful human relationships are — through sustained attention, carefully placed tension, and the willingness to press against each other until something real emerges.



We don't do campaigns. We cultivate conditions. A single encounter is rarely enough. What we build together is meant to linger — in the mind, in the body, in the decision.
